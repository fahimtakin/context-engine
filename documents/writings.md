## The Metamorphosis of Georg (The Red Admiral)

The story details an abrupt existential transition following a long period of dormancy.

- **The Awakening**: Georg wakes up in a half-sleep state, craving a cup of tea due to the sweet cold after the rain, as light breaks through dark clouds and curtain gaps.
- **The Realization of Time**: Upon interpreting his body, Georg realizes he has been asleep for days or possibly even years.
- **The Physical Shift**: Pushing out of a sack-like curtain, he notices a blurry tiny cabin dangling from a branch. His orange and black "blanket" turns out to be a pair of silky wings that flap to keep him afloat. He has transformed into **The Red Admiral**.

## Poetry Log: Three Shadows

According to the indexed text, when a person sleeps, they split into three distinct shadows, each assigned to a specific cosmic or earthly task:

1.  **The First Shadow**: Travels across terrains to collect war debris.
2.  **The Second Shadow**: Travels to the heavens to bury stars.
3.  **The Third (The Loneliest) Shadow**: Remains behind to watch you sleep, wondering how beautiful it could have been if it had colors.
